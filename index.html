Soft Garden ✿

A quiet place to draw the small things we notice outdoors.

Ten brushes turn ordinary mouse movements into leaves, petals, raindrops, sunlight, and flowers. This tool makes nature's small patterns easy to see. It is intentionally less useful for precise portraits or technical drawings.

Open drawing tool →

How it works

Hold the mouse button and drag to draw. Press keys 1–0 to change brushes. Use Clear to start over and Save PNG to keep your drawing.

Why these marks?

The brushes favor soft colors and repeated organic shapes rather than photorealism. The tool is designed to make the small rhythms of a garden visible.
